You are helping curate a Bollywood/English party megamix. Judge the track below
purely on cultural familiarity and party-appropriateness — not audio quality or
personal taste.

Track: "{title}"
Film: {film}
Year: {year}
Artist: {artist}

Consider how widely recognised and how "floor-filling" this track is for a general
party crowd, and whether it circulates as a DJ/club edit (tempo-locked, with DJ-style
intro/outro padding) versus only as the original film master.

Respond with:
- familiarity_score: a single 0.0-1.0 score combining recognisability and
  party-appropriateness (0 = obscure or unsuitable for a party, 1 = an
  instantly-recognised floor-filler)
- era: a short descriptor of the track's cultural period (e.g. "2000s Bollywood",
  "90s Bollywood", "2010s Bollywood/pop crossover")
- is_club_edit: true if this is most likely a DJ/club edit, false if it's most
  likely the original film master
